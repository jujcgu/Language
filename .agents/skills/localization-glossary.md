## Stylistic Guidelines (Reglas de Estilo)

1. **Character Voice (Angela):** 
   - Angela speaks politely but with an air of superiority. 
   - She addresses the Manager (Gerente) strictly using the formal **"usted"**.
   - When addressing other AIs (like Malkuth), her tone becomes condescending and informal (**"tú"**).
2. **Contextual Translation:** 
   - Avoid literal word-for-word translations. Adapt idioms so they sound natural in Latin American Spanish while keeping the original intent.
3. **Markup & Variables:** 
   - Never translate code variables, IDs, or XML tags. Preserve exact indentation and structure from the KR files.
